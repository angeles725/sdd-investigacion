# <SUBJECT> — Contradictions ledger

> Corpus-level home for UNRESOLVED contradictions and open questions: "source A says X,
> source B says Y, and I cannot yet adjudicate" (METHODOLOGY §14). Twin of the gap-backlog
> in RESEARCH-STATE. A conflict lands here as `open` INSTEAD of being force-collapsed into a
> premature `[INFER]` or silently dropped. `research-sdd-status.sh` surfaces the `open` count
> in its status report so it is not forgotten at STOP. When a side finally wins, set the row to
> `resolved`, record the winner in `note`, and CORRECT the losing block per §14 (keep the
> original text, add a "corrected in BN" note + the new citation).
