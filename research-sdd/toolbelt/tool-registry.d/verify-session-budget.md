### verify-session-budget

| Tool | Description |
|---|---|
| `verify-session-budget.sh` — mixed worst case (kit issue #2756) | `rsdd budget session` adds `mixed` lines to the Claude report, after the `aggregate[<source>]:` line of each source and after the overall `aggregate:` line. They name the population each figure samples: `aggregate*` is the NORMAL total (every hook at its measured size); `mixed[<source>]: <n> chars (budget < 8000) mixed-verdict=OK\|OVER (normal total <n>, all-fail <hooks> x 800 = <n>) — normal: <hook> <chars>, ...; cap 800: <hook>, ...` is the mixed worst case; the all-fail figure is hooks x `hookenv.FailureCap`. The mixed worst case is the SUM over the source's hooks of max(normal size, `hookenv.FailureCap`): a hook that fails hands the model a bounded failure context, which costs the cap only when its normal context is smaller. The line names which hooks contribute their normal figure and which the cap. A degraded hook (no measured size) is counted at the cap and the line says `(partial: K degraded, counted at the cap)`; the overall line `mixed: <n> chars ... mixed-verdict=... worst-source=<source>` carries `(partial: K degraded)`. The figure is a READING built from measured normal contexts, not a bound: it becomes one only when every hook declares a success-path maximum (kit issue #2773); the overall line says so. The lines never start with `aggregate` and never contain `OVER BUDGET` (their verdict words are `OK`/`OVER`), so the `verify-registry.sh` session-budget checks, which parse `^aggregate: ` and `OVER BUDGET`, are unchanged. `--harness pi` prints `mixed: not computed for pi (...)`: the pi extension caps its whole injected string at 7900 characters itself. The gate is `TestMixedGateOnFrozenFixture` (`internal/budget`): it computes the mixed worst case through the same function from the frozen, dated reading `testdata/session-normal-2026-11-06.txt` and requires it under 8,000, and requires the fixture to name exactly the SessionStart hooks `.claude/settings.json` registers (a hook added without a reading, or a reading of a hook no longer registered, fails). Intended divergence from the frozen bash oracle (`testdata/legacy/verify-session-budget.sh`): the `mixed` lines, removed by one test helper before the differential comparison; the goldens under `tests/fixtures/verify-session-budget/_frozen/` carry them. Existing lines are byte-identical. |
